In a lot of our families, aesthetic treatments are either a secret or a scandal.

Either you do not tell anyone what you got done — because the judgment is not worth it — or someone finds out and suddenly you are doing too much or trying to be something you are not.

Meanwhile, the same aunties who will side-eye your Botox appointment have been swearing by their Vaseline routine and their grandmother's skin secrets for decades. Because taking care of how you look has always mattered. It just had to look effortless. Admitting you got help was somehow the problem.

That is changing. And it should.

Botox and aesthetic treatments are not exclusively for wealthy white women in Boston. They never were. But for too long, the clinics that offered them were not in our neighborhoods, did not look like us, did not understand our skin tones, our features, or our beauty standards. So we either did not go, or we went somewhere that was not equipped to serve us well, and we walked away with results that confirmed every fear we had.

RefynMe exists because that gap is unacceptable.

Your face deserves precision. Your skin tone — whether deep brown, warm gold, or anywhere in between — deserves a provider who understands how neuromodulators and fillers behave differently across different skin types and facial structures. Not every face is the same, and cookie-cutter treatments produce cookie-cutter results.

We understand this community. And we believe every woman who walks through our door deserves to leave feeling more like herself — not less.

No judgment. No pressure. Just an honest conversation about what is possible.
